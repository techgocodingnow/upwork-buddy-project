UpworkBuddy

A lightweight macOS menu-bar app that shows your Upwork freelance earnings at a glance — hours, earnings, and per-project breakdowns rolled up by today, week, month, and year.

Download for macOS View on GitHub

Why UpworkBuddy

  • Lives in the menu barNo Dock icon, no clutter — just your numbers a click away.
  • Private by designOAuth tokens are stored securely in the macOS Keychain, never on a server.
  • Zero dependenciesPure Swift + SwiftPM. Open source under the MIT license.
  • Auto-updatingShips signed, notarized releases with EdDSA-verified Sparkle updates.

Requirements

macOS 14 (Sonoma) or newer, and an Upwork developer app for OAuth. Full setup instructions are in the README.
